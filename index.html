SaltWërk is an early-stage performance gear startup founded and owned by Daren Mann, a Santa Barbara-based entrepreneur and marathon runner. Our flagship product, TrailGuard, is a compression calf sleeve designed for trail runners and hikers, combining performance compression with multi-threat protection for the trail: including snake-resistant materials, permethrin-based tick protection, and abrasion resistance.

We will be beginning pre-orders and delivering the first products in Q1 2027.
